**The case for**

2. Product maturity (4/4) — Exchange in production well over 12 months (token genesis Nov 2024 on a live venue); HyperEVM since Feb 2025, HIP-3 since Oct 2025.
3. Token utility & value accrual (4/4) — 99% of fees (net of builder fees) buy HYPE on-chain — $1.36B spent so far; HYPE also needed for staking, gas and HIP-3 deployment bonds.

**The case against**

1. Licensing for regulated activity (0/4) — Offers retail leveraged perps (up to 40×) with no licence anywhere; FCA warning list, MAS alert; ESMA caps EU retail at 2:1.
2. Audit remediation (1/4) — The only public audits (two Zellic reports, 2023) cover the legacy bridge; no public audit of HyperCore/HyperEVM, which hold most of the ~$8.4B.
3. Admin key custody (1/4) — No admin key, but bridge withdrawals need only 3 of 4 EOA validator signatures, and L1 upgrades ship as binaries adopted by a validator set where the Foundation holds ~49% of stake.
